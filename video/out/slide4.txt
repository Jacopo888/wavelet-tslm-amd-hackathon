Then we asked the hard question: does it transfer to a real plant, with no training on it? We used HAI twenty-one point oh three, an industrial control testbed with fifty documented attacks. Thirty-eight windows, each nine hours and sixteen channels, one call each. The model touched thirty-four of the fifty attacks. Placing the same intervals at random would touch about seven. And sixty percent of its intervals land on an attack, against four and a half percent by chance.
